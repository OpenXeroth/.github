# OpenXeroth

Open tools for understanding wildlife.

OpenXeroth is part of [Xeroth](https://xeroth.ai). We share tools and research using optics, acoustics and AI to support wildlife observation and conservation field work. Explore the projects at **[open.xeroth.ai](https://open.xeroth.ai)**.

## Explore

- **[NatureCam](https://open.xeroth.ai/naturecam/)** brings nature cameras, human expertise and AI together, starting at Djuma Cam. Hosana, our individual animal re-identification pipeline, and AI assistance for volunteer camera operators are in development.
- **[Djuma birds](https://open.xeroth.ai/djuma-birds/)** lets anyone browse and listen to bird-call detections from Djuma Cam, analysed with BirdNET-Cloud. These are automated species suggestions, with geographic checks, rather than confirmed sightings or individual identities.
- **[Acoustic re-identification research](https://open.xeroth.ai/acoustic-re-id-research/)** examines how recording conditions can inflate apparent accuracy when AI identifies individual animals. [Read the code and reproducibility notes](https://github.com/OpenXeroth/acoustic-re-id-research).
